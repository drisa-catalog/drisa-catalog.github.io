How to derive

From map to catalog in four steps.

  1. Download the maps

    Accept the rules and download the seven pieces at 512 × 512.

  2. Paint your texture

    Use the map as a guide in your editor. Keep 512 × 512 and make both color and opacity. The tattoo layers sit over the skin, so leave clear areas transparent.

  3. Derive in IMVU Studio

    Derive from the REGATA DRISA product and replace the textures of the materials in the table below. Material 7 is the skin and follows the skin the avatar wears.

  4. Test and publish

    Check it on the avatar, adjust the opacity and submit it to the catalog.

Usage rules

The textures are yours. The mesh is mine.

Create and sell as much as you like on top of this tank top, as long as it is by derivation in the IMVU catalog.

You may

  • Derive this tank top through the IMVU catalog and sell your versions there.
  • Create and sell your own textures: tank top prints, tattoos and nails.

  • Use the mesh in other games or platforms.
  • Edit the mesh to hide where it came from.
  • Resell these maps as a pack or resource.

If it is stolen

What happens to anyone who copies the mesh.

  1. Identification

    The hidden signature and the mesh comparison confirm the copy.

  2. DMCA report

    I send IMVU a formal copyright report with the proof of authorship.

  3. Product removal

    With a valid report, IMVU removes or disables the product.

  4. Account at risk

    IMVU may close accounts that repeat infringements. A permanently disabled account is unlikely to withdraw its earnings.

I may also seek compensation in court under copyright law (Law 9.610/98 in Brazil).
