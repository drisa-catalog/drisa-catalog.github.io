Usage rules

The textures are yours. The mesh is mine.

Create and sell as much as you like on top of this head, as long as it is by derivation in the IMVU catalog.

You may

  • Derive this head through the IMVU catalog and sell your versions there.
  • Create and sell your own textures: mask, makeup, eyebrows and lashes.
  • Use the maps on this page to paint your textures.
  • Promote your derived products with your own images.

You may not

  • Extract, copy or re-upload the mesh as .chkn, .xmf, .obj or any other format.
  • Publish the mesh as your own creation or as a non-derived product.
  • Sell, trade or share the mesh and its files outside the IMVU catalog.
  • Use the mesh in other games or platforms.
  • Edit the mesh to hide where it came from.
  • Resell these maps as a pack or resource.

By downloading the maps you agree to these rules.

I keep the dated source files of the mesh. The original file has a unique fingerprint, and any copy can be checked against it.

Point-by-point comparison

A suspicious mesh is compared with the original, vertex by vertex. Edited copies usually keep the same structure, and the comparison shows it.

If it is stolen

What happens to anyone who copies the mesh.

  1. Identification

    The hidden signature and the mesh comparison confirm the copy.

  2. DMCA report

    I send IMVU a formal copyright report with the proof of authorship.

  3. Product removal

    With a valid report, IMVU removes or disables the product.

  4. Account at risk

    IMVU may close accounts that repeat infringements. A permanently disabled account is unlikely to withdraw its earnings.

I may also seek compensation in court under copyright law (Law 9.610/98 in Brazil).
